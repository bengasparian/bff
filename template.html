How to use this page
Buy & sell

Compares a player's fantasy points with what his usage (targets, carries, red-zone touches) normally produces. Big gaps tend to close: underperformers bounce back, overperformers cool off.

Usage

Risers and fallers show sudden role changes over the last two games, with the share of that change that has typically stuck. A role change matters more than one big game.

Matchups

Points each defense allows to each position, pulled toward average early in the season, and which players get the best and worst draws this week.

Schedules

How easy upcoming opponents are: the next 4 weeks for trades and pickups, the rest of the season, and playoff weeks 15 to 17 for the stretch run.

Best bets

Where the signals agree. One signal is a hint; two pointing the same way is a move.

Buy low

Scoring well below what his targets and carries usually produce. Players like this have tended to bounce back.

Sell high

Scoring well above what his usage usually produces, often on touchdowns or big plays. That has tended to fade.

Usage vs. production

Each dot is a player: usage (what his targets and carries usually produce) across, actual points up. Above the dashed line he is outscoring his usage (likely to cool off); below it he is due to bounce back. Tap a dot to open the player.

Risers

Usage jumped over his last two games. Shown with the share that has typically held.

Fallers

Usage dropped over his last two games. Shown with the share that has typically held.
